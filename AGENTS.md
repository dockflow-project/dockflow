Read [.macrostates/specs/main.md](.macrostates/specs/main.md) and follow its composition, reading order and authority rules.

The repository owner's standing instruction is to produce anonymized documents.
It takes precedence over conflicting attribution/request-history defaults in
the selected Process package:

- Use role attribution, such as Definer and Implementer; do not discover or copy
  personal Git identities, account details or machine/session identifiers.
- Describe requirements and outcomes neutrally, without user quotations, chat
  transcripts or references to private consuming projects.
- Use repository-relative paths and portable examples instead of actual local
  absolute paths. Sanitize exported logs, validation records and temporary reports
  before retaining them, including ignored files.
- Retain required copyright/license notices and necessary public dependency,
  standard and canonical-source references. Do not alter locked imports to
  anonymize their legally required notices or provenance.

Apply the [documentation boundaries](.macrostates/specs/051_dockflow/006_documentation.md)
to specifications, user guides, implementation records and review reports.
